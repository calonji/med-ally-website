## Frequently Asked Questions About MedAlly Pricing

### What MedAlly plans are available?

MedAlly offers **Forever Free, Professional, Ultimate, and Enterprise**.

### How much is Forever Free?

Forever Free is **$0 USD** and includes **10 clinical encounters per month**.

### How much is Professional?

Professional is **$49 USD/mo per clinician** and includes **unlimited encounters**, Advanced multilingual scribe, Medical coding maps (ICD-10), and Basic HIPAA storage.

### How much is Ultimate?

Ultimate is **$99 USD/mo per clinician** and includes **all Professional features**, plus Predictive clinical support, 200+ guideline scans, Treatment plan assistance, and Priority AI latency.

### How much is Enterprise?

Enterprise uses **custom pricing** and is designed for **clinical teams and custom systems**.

### What counts as a MedAlly encounter?

One MedAlly session counts as one encounter.

### Do the 10 free encounters renew every month?

Yes. Forever Free renews with **10 free encounters every month**.

### What happens after I use all 10 free encounters?

A **Professional, Ultimate, or Enterprise subscription** is required to start additional encounters before the next monthly renewal.

If you remain on Forever Free, your allowance renews to **10 free encounters at the next monthly reset**.

### Are Professional and Ultimate monthly subscriptions?

Yes. Professional is **$49 USD/mo per clinician** and Ultimate is **$99 USD/mo per clinician**.

### Can I cancel Professional or Ultimate?

Yes. **Professional and Ultimate use flexible monthly agreements and can be canceled anytime.**

For the exact cancellation effective date or billing treatment, follow the current checkout/billing terms. Enterprise uses custom commercial terms.

### What is the difference between Professional and Ultimate?

Professional includes unlimited encounters and the Professional feature set. Ultimate includes all Professional features and adds Predictive clinical support, 200+ guideline scans, Treatment plan assistance, and Priority AI latency.

### Who is Enterprise for?

Enterprise is for clinical teams and custom systems that need a tailored deployment, including the Enterprise capabilities listed above.
